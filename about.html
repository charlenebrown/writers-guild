1996 — 2026 / A COMMUNITY WITH ROOTS

Thirty years.
Many next chapters.

A literary association imagined around a lasting commitment: Black, African American, and Caribbean stories belong at the center of the conversation.

OUR PURPOSE

Make room.
Make it matter.

We connect writers across generations and places through craft development, public programs, and a shared belief in the cultural value of the written word.

Our work begins with Black and Caribbean experience and grows through thoughtful collaboration. We welcome Latino, Asian, and other writers into a community where difference creates conversation, not decoration.

THE ASSOCIATION STORY

Built through
the work.

1996

A circle becomes a commitment.

The association’s imagined founding begins with a small Brooklyn writing circle and a promise to keep returning to the page.

2004

A wider literary conversation.

Regional connections bring writers from Louisiana, Atlanta, Florida, and other communities into shared workshops and readings.

2012

Opportunity becomes part of the practice.

Mentorship, institutional collaboration, and fellowship concepts deepen the association’s focus on access.

2020

The room reaches beyond the room.

Online circles make sustained literary conversation possible across distance and changing circumstances.

2026

Thirty years. Still building.

The anniversary gathering looks forward: more voices, deeper craft, and partnerships that give writing a public life.

This 30-year history is fictional concept content, not the history of an existing organization.
