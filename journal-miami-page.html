PLACE & PRACTICE

Miami is more than a backdrop.

Simone Laurent · Oct 05, 2026 · 5 min read

A city made of conversations.

My Miami is a place where a conversation can change languages halfway through a sentence and still be completely understood. Writing it requires attention to the person speaking, not just the words that look striking on the page.

Move beyond the postcard.

The most useful details are often ordinary: the wait for the bus, a family errand, a song through an open window. A city becomes a place in fiction when people want something inside it.

Write the distance honestly.

Migration does not stop when a plane lands. It appears in habits, silences, expectations, and the versions of home people keep. I try to make room for that ongoing movement without turning every character into a lesson.

Find a room that understands the work.

Our Florida writers connect across Miami, Orlando, and other communities. The circle offers space to test scenes, talk about bilingual voice, and learn from writers whose relationship to the state is different from our own.
