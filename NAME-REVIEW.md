# Preliminary proposed-name review

**Proposed name:** Top Writers Guild Association  
**Research date:** October 8, 2026  
**Decision:** Do not represent the name as available or cleared. The concept uses “Writers Association Concept” pending a more complete review.

## What was checked

- Exact web searches for “Top Writers Guild Association” and “Top Writers Guild” did not surface an exact organization match in the returned results. Search coverage is incomplete, and this is not proof that no organization or common-law trademark exists.
- `.com` registry RDAP requests for `topwritersguildassociation.com` and `topwritersguild.com` returned HTTP 404 (no registration record returned at the time of lookup):
  - https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/topwritersguildassociation.com
  - https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/topwritersguild.com
- The `.org` RDAP request returned HTTP 403, so its registration status could not be verified:
  - https://rdap.publicinterestregistry.org/rdap/domain/topwritersguildassociation.org
- USPTO search and clearance guidance was reviewed. A comprehensive search of the official trademark database for exact and confusingly similar marks was **not completed**, and there is no trademark clearance opinion.

## What remains

Confirm domain registration availability with a registrar immediately before purchase. Search the USPTO trademark database, state registries, relevant international records, organization/business-name records, common-law uses, and similar marks in related services. Review potential confusion with existing writers' guilds and associations. Domain registration by itself does not confer trademark clearance.

Official guidance:
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks

The neutral descriptive heading used in this portfolio is not asserted to be a cleared trademark either. No affiliation with any real writers' guild or literary organization is claimed.
